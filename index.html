Brazos HealthLens · Where are chronic disease, risk and care gaps heaviest?

Linked views of CDC PLACES model estimates for all 6,844 Texas census tracts, focused on the 7-county Brazos Valley. Select a tract or brush a group in any view and it lights up in every view. Darker colors always mean worse.

How the selection compares: county averages

Adult-population-weighted averages for the focus measure. Brazos Valley counties in bold; Texas and Valley reference bars. Click a bar to highlight that county's tracts.

Parallel coordinates: many measures of every tract at once

One line per tract. Every axis is oriented so up = worse (care measures are flipped). Drag on an axis to brush a range (several brushes combine with AND); drag an axis title to reorder; double-click an axis title to remove it.

Overplotting: lines are semi-transparent; brushed lines are drawn on top in blue. In "All Texas" mode 6,844 lines are drawn; brush to filter.

Relationship explorer

Each dot is a tract, sized by population. Dashed line = least-squares fit across tracts in scope. Y follows the focus measure. Drag to brush and highlight a group; click a dot to select it.

Strongest relationships with the Y measure

r measures linear association across tracts, not causation. All PLACES measures are modeled from the same demographic inputs, so part of every correlation is built into the model, and tract-level associations do not describe individuals.

What makes each health profile group different

Rows = groups (k-means on 23 standardized measures), columns = measures. Color = group mean in Texas standard deviations, signed so brown = worse than the Texas tract average and teal = better (care measures are flipped). Hover for the raw mean; click a row to filter that group.

Unusual for their group

Care gaps: high disease burden + weak preventive care

Burden = mean Texas z-score of diabetes, high BP, heart disease, stroke and COPD. Care deficit = mean z of uninsured (18–64), no routine checkup, not taking BP medicine (adults with high BP) and no cholesterol screening. Tertile classes from the scope. Darkest = high on both.

Burden vs. care deficit

Lines = tertile cut points. Click a legend cell on the map to highlight that class.
